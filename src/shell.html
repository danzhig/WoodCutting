Using the handbook
  • Each topic is one page. Turn pages with the Previous/Next buttons at the bottom, the ← → keys, or a swipe on a phone.
  • Contents (top left) lists every guide and topic, and searches titles, terms and text.
  • Print (top right) prints this page, one guide, or the whole handbook on white paper.
  • The handbook remembers where you stopped. All sizes are in inches, using real (actual) lumber sizes.
